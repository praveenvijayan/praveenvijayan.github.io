How it works.

No discovery theatre. Three steps from first call to agreed scope.

  1. Call

    15 or 30 minutes. You describe the system, the team, and the constraint. I say plainly what I think, including when I am not the right person.

  2. Proposal

    Within two working days: scope, deliverables, timeline, price, and what I need from your side. One page, no slide deck.

  3. Work

    Weekly written updates. Architecture decisions recorded as we go. Everything handed over in your repositories and your documentation, not mine.

A good fit when

  • You run software in a regulated or high-stakes environment and the usual AI demos do not survive your review process.
  • You need someone who has led delivery with large teams and can also open the editor and build.
  • You want AI-assisted engineering adopted across a team, with the guardrails and measurement to show it is helping.
  • You are in the GCC, or your working day overlaps with Gulf Standard Time.

Probably not when

  • You need a vendor to pick a chatbot platform and nothing more.
  • The brief is a research prototype with no path to production.
