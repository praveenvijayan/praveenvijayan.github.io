Research with explicit boundaries.

Two independent preprints on how software evolves and how identity is established.

Position paper · 31 August 2026

Governed Software Evolution

Embedded Engineering Agents and the Curation of User-Originated Variability in Enterprise Applications

An architecture proposal for applications that users can extend through embedded engineering agents. It examines permissions, extension contracts, and the review process that could move personal improvements into team and enterprise capabilities.

Preprint. Proposed architecture and evaluation plan; the paper reports no implemented system or empirical results.

Preprint · 9 August 2026

Earned Identity

Anonymous-First Authentication with Lazy Passkey Elevation for Low-Friction Web Applications

An identity lifecycle that lets people use an application before registering, then preserve their anonymous identity through a passkey when they have something worth keeping.

Includes a protocol specification, security and privacy analyses, and comparisons with existing authentication approaches.
